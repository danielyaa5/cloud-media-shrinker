# Cloud Media Shrinker

Sort your photos and videos by size to find what’s taking up the most space. Compress the files you want to keep, or delete the ones you no longer need.

Review your Apple Photos library and folders on iPhone, iPad, and Mac. On Mac, you can also review folders from Google Drive for desktop.

**[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/cloud-media-shrinker/id6824383256)**

Requires macOS 13 or later, iOS 27 or later, or iPadOS 27 or later. The App Store lists the price for your region.

## Find your biggest files first

Browse Apple Photos from largest to smallest, with file sizes, capture dates, and previews on each tile. Filter by media type or year, and hide items you have kept or already compressed.

![The library, sorted largest first](docs/screenshot-library.png)

## Choose how much to compress

Use Balanced, Maximum Savings, or Preserve 4K presets in Simple mode. Advanced mode gives you control over resolution, frame rate, format, and quality. Compress supported videos, RAW and JPEG photos, or make a still-only copy of a Live Photo.

![Compression presets and estimated output sizes](docs/screenshot-shrink.png)

## Select a batch in one step

Auto Select finds items above a size you choose in the current view. Review the selection before starting. Compression batches account for the room your device needs to download originals.

![Auto Select with a minimum file size](docs/screenshot-auto-select.png)

## Preview before deciding

Play videos and seek through the timeline before choosing what to keep. For media stored only in iCloud, the preview can take a few minutes to arrive; the app shows download progress when Photos provides it.

![Video preview with playback controls](docs/screenshot-preview.png)

## Apple Photos, Google Drive, and local folders

Review your Photos library or choose a folder on your device. On Mac, you can also select a folder on an external drive or one that Google Drive for desktop shows in Finder. Folder compression saves smaller copies in a Compressed folder inside the folder you chose.

![A Google Drive folder with the current interface](docs/screenshot-drive.png)

## Good to know

- Compression runs on your device. The app does not upload your library to the developer.
- Compression is lossy, and savings vary. Check a smaller copy before deleting its original.
- Apple Photos deletions go to Recently Deleted; folder deletions go to the relevant Trash. Cloud storage may not be reclaimed until originals leave Recently Deleted or the service’s trash.
- An original stored in iCloud downloads to your device before compression. Leave room for both the original and its smaller copy.
- Each device keeps its own Savings history and Keep decisions.

## Get started

1. Download Cloud Media Shrinker from the App Store on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.
2. Open the app and choose the photos or folders you want it to review.
3. Allow access to your Photos library or the folders you selected. To review Google Drive files, choose a Drive folder on Mac.
4. Review your largest items and choose what to compress, keep, or delete.
